# Captain Sonar Shared Game Rules Context

## Game Overview

Captain Sonar is a team-based submarine duel. Two teams coordinate inside their own submarines while trying to locate and damage the enemy. Communication between the four roles is essential.

## Winning Condition

- The first submarine to reach 4 damage is destroyed.
- The surviving team wins.

## Team Structure

- Each team has four roles: Captain, First Mate, Engineer, and Radio Operator.
- In smaller games, one player may cover multiple roles.

## Turn Structure

- In turn-by-turn mode, teams alternate turns.
- The Captain cannot announce a new course until the First Mate and Engineer finish their tasks for the previous move.
- A team cannot activate two systems in a row without a movement announcement between them.

## Captain Movement Rules

- The Captain moves one space at a time: north, south, east, or west.
- The submarine cannot cross its own route.
- The submarine cannot move into islands.
- The submarine cannot move into a space containing its own mine.
- If no legal course exists, the Captain must surface immediately.

## First Mate Rules

- The First Mate fills gauges by marking spaces after course announcements.
- When a gauge fills, the corresponding system becomes ready.
- The First Mate informs the Captain when a system is ready.
- The First Mate can activate drone and sonar.

## Engineer Rules

- The Engineer crosses out a symbol for each Captain course announcement.
- Some crossed symbols disable systems.
- If all symbols for a system are crossed, that system cannot be activated.
- Radiation or complete area breakdowns can cause damage.
- Surfacing repairs breakdowns.

## Systems

- Torpedo: attack a target 1 to 4 spaces away, orthogonally.
- Mine: place a mine adjacent to the submarine on a legal tile.
- Trigger Mine: detonate a previously placed mine.
- Drone: check whether the enemy is in a sector.
- Sonar: reveal one true and one false piece of positional information.
- Silence: move up to four spaces without announcing the route.
- Scenario: special rules may add scenario-specific powers.

## Surfacing

- Surfacing repairs breakdowns and resets the route.
- The Captain announces the current sector.
- Surfacing is risky because it leaves the submarine vulnerable.

## Role Coordination

- The Captain chooses movement and combat timing.
- The First Mate manages readiness and activation timing.
- The Engineer protects the team from harmful breakdowns.
- The Radio Operator tracks enemy position and narrows targets.

## Scenario Notes

- Different maps may change how drones, sectors, or surfacing behave.
- Some scenarios add special rules such as Archer mine control.
